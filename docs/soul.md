# The soul model automation assumes

`;soul` (scripts/soul.py, model in client/game/soul.py) keeps a Paladin's soul up and reads it, because the circle-5 glyph quest's orb refuses a soul below pristine and a pool below full, and `;hunt`'s SMITE with an empty pool is what drained it (#217). Canon is [Elanthipedia's Soul system](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Soul_system); this is what our code believes and why, from the readings of 2026-09-18 and 2026-09-19.

## The two numbers

1. **The state**, seven levels read by RUB on a soulstone or the guild's orb, or by walking through a soulstone arch: black and corrupted, cold and lifeless grey, pallid grey, chalky grey, steady white hue, pure white light, pristine luminescence. It drifts down with time but never below chalky grey on its own. `soul.parse_state` reads the level off the phrase; the orb and the tower's arch use the same words ("It fades to a dull, chalky color." / "The archway emits a warm, steady white hue!").
2. **The pool**, eleven levels read by EXHALE, sized by circle, Charisma and the state — so every boost of the state makes the pool read emptier until it refills. Empty is "Nothing special seems to happen." (captured, not on the wiki's list); the second level "You catch the faintest flicker of light." `soul.parse_pool` matches the most specific phrase first because the wiki's lines nest ("briefly flickers" inside "flickers with an inner light").

## The orb's refusal

FOCUS ORB in the Tower of Honor's Orb Room (map 8228) answered "You attempt to focus on the orb, but you feel you need to rest and contemplate a bit first." at chalky grey with the pool empty (2026-09-18), at steady white with the pool at 2/11, and again at 04:35 on 2026-09-19 with the orb reading pristine ("It gleams brightly with a pristine luminescence!") and the pool full ("It brightens with a powerful inner light that, somehow, manages to not cast shadows."), the quest given by Verika the day before. So the line is not the soul gate: a 2012 forum thread asked the same question with a pristine soul and the quest in hand, and the answer was "a sort of wait-a-bit message" (tinyheroes.com, Holy Glyphs, 07/19/2012) — a timer, most likely restarted by every failed FOCUS, of unmeasured length; the walkthrough's soul requirement and dr-scripts' "You focus your magical senses" refusal stand beside it. `ready_for_quest` is pristine and full, `;soul quest` reads both before it FOCUSes (`force` skips the check), and after a "rest and contemplate" the thing to do is not try again soon: the retries tonight came 1 h 46 min and 3 h 49 min after the previous failure, both refused.

## The deeds and their timers

1. **The tithe**: PUT 5 silver of the town's coin in the almsbox — Dokoras in Shard's Temple of Light, Alcove of Smaragdaus (map 13143, tagged `tithe`); the box's inscription says so and more coins give no more. Captured: "You drop 5 silver dokoras into the almsbox and say a soft prayer as the coins clink in." / "A warm, soothing sensation washes over your soul." One tithe moved the state from chalky grey to steady white hue and the pool from empty to the second level. Timer: four hours (the wiki). The map tags two almsbox rooms (Shard's, Ratha's guild foyer); the Crossing's two are `soul.ALMSBOXES` (read off the rooms 2026-09-20): "the locked almsbox" outside the temple gate (Immortals' Approach, map 741) and "a steel tithe box" outside the Paladins' Guild (Herald Street, map 815), whose inscription reads "To donate: PUT [amount] [coin type] KRONARS IN BOX" — so the PUT names `box` there and `almsbox` elsewhere, chosen off the room's listing (`soul.box_noun`). `almsbox=ID` names any other. A character who owes the province is refused: "That's very altruistic, but you should really pay off your debt before making any donations." (captured 2026-09-24, #304). `;soul` reads WEALTH's Debt section before the PUT, says the debt once (";debt pays it") and backs off; while the mark stands a WEALTH with no walk decides the next try, and a WEALTH showing the debt paid clears it.
2. **The prayer**: PRAY CHADATRU at his altar or statue — the Tower of Honor's chapel (13430), the Crossing temple's Chadatru's Shrine (5845), the rooms the map tags `chadatru`. The first answer, "As you kneel down to pray, you feel your head is not cleared enough to pay proper respect to Chadatru.", is the prayer beginning: stay knelt about 75 seconds for "After clearing your thoughts, you pray deeply toward Chadatru.  A warm, soothing sensation washes over your soul." (captured 2026-09-19 02:34 through `;soul pray`). Hands must be empty. A second try answers "You start to pay respect to Chadatru again when you decide it would be inappropriate so soon.  You decide to wait awhile longer." — at 6, 10 and 74 minutes after the first attempt, and the prayer went through at 120 minutes, so the timer is two hours from the attempt, not dr-scripts' hour; a refusal backs off twenty minutes. The plain prayer (without the circle-50 Glyph of Renewal) is a full step: the orb read "It hums softly and emits a pure white light!" right after it (6/7, from steady white) and the pool "It pulses with an inner light." (6/11, from 2/11 two hours earlier) — so the pool refills within hours, not days.
3. **The pilgrim's badge**: PRAY BADGE is a soul boost on a 31-minute timer, the most repeatable one, growing with the altars and shrines pushed onto the badge (PUSH <altar> WITH BADGE while holding it; Elanthipedia: Pilgrim's badge). The plain badge is 37,500 Kronars on the onyx tray in Brother Durantine's storeroom in the Crossing (map 19073, `go store`), which only a Cleric may enter: "Brother Durantine beams as he ushers you into his storeroom to see his latest offerings. "Welcome, Brother, ...""; ORDER BADGE buys it outright ("You decide to purchase the badge, and pay the sales clerk 37500 Kronars. / The sales clerk hands you your pilgrim's badge.") and a GIVE hands it over ("You offer your pilgrim's badge to <name>, who has 30 seconds to accept the offer." / "You accept <name>'s offer and are now holding a pilgrim's badge.") — captured 2026-09-20 with the operator's Cleric buying for the Paladin. A new badge is bonded with a kiss ("You press the badge to your lips, making it your own."; before that an altar answers "You have not yet made this badge your own." and PRAY BADGE "This is not your pilgrim's badge."), and only the altars the wiki lists as attuned count: Chadatru's Shrine in the Crossing temple answered PUSH GRANITE ALTAR WITH BADGE with "You press your pilgrim's badge against the altar, and you feel a shiver of holy power tremor through it into you. / Though you feel a burst of holy energy course through you, the keepers of the shrine do not appear to have aligned it with the pilgrim's association.", and PRAY BADGE with nothing on it "You think really hard about your badge.  It doesn't do anything though." (all captured 2026-09-20). With four attuned sites pushed — the Resurrection Creche's altar, the Shrine of Kertigen's statue, Tamsine's Rest's image, the Shrine of Ushnish's image (each "You press your pilgrim's badge against the <noun>, and you feel a shiver of holy power tremor through it into you.") — PRAY BADGE answered "As you feel your connection to them grow, you sense the eyes of the gods upon you. / Roundtime: 10 sec. / A warm, soothing sensation washes over your soul. / You feel a strengthening of your faith and bolstering of your soul." `;soul badge` is that deed — REMOVE MY BADGE (it is worn: "You put on a pilgrim's badge."; GET it when it is not), PRAY BADGE, WEAR it again, wherever the character stands — and `;soul keep` runs it every thirty-one minutes beside the tithe and the prayer; no badge on the character turns it off for the run.
4. **Not automated**: tending a non-Paladin's wounds and guarding one against a creature (hourly, need a second character), undead kills (random), the instrument and altar rituals that want ranks a circle-5 Paladin lacks.

The timers live in `~/.revenant/soul/<name>.json` (the last acceptance and the last refusal per deed), so a restarted `;soul keep` does not tithe twice. A deed's room more than 80 rooms away is skipped, not walked to: a keep in Shard must not set off for the Crossing's boxes, nor the other way round.

The readings (`;soul` alone) RUB and EXHALE an orb in the room; with none — the Crossing guild has no orb — the state comes off the nearest soulstone arch (`soul.ARCHES`: the guild's Chambers `go arch` into the Hallway, Shard's `go tower` from Xibar's Crescent Road), stepped through for "The archway gleams with a pristine luminescence in welcome!" (2026-09-20), the same words as a RUB (#231); the pool has no reading short of Shard's Orb Room. A state reading is kept in the timers file (`read`, `state`) for four hours.

**The pristine gate** (the operator, 2026-09-20): while a reading younger than four hours says pristine, `;soul keep` and `;train`'s rests run no deed at all — the deeds restore a soul, they do not maintain one, and the wiki's soul drift is slow and never takes the soul below chalky grey on its own. With no fresh reading the reading comes first (`;train` runs `;soul read` in the rest, then walks back); a reading that fails (no arch within 80 rooms, an answer the table does not know) backs off twenty minutes and the deeds run on the old rule meanwhile. Below pristine the deeds run on their timers as before.

## What lowers it

The wiki's list, and `;hunt`'s part of it: fleeing combat (the walker's RETREAT burst on the mudflats), and SMITE with an empty pool — one evening of it took the soul from wherever it was to chalky grey and the pool to nothing (#217). A pool reading before each smite is the fix there.
