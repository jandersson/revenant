# Wounds

`client/client/game/wounds.py` turns a HEALTH answer into numbers: each body area gets four severities on a 1–8 scale, plus the bleeding table. Scripts compare those numbers rather than the health percentage — `;tend` takes its bleeders from it, `;hunt` stops at the profile's wound floor.

## The scale

| Level | Name | Sample wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | insignificant | "minor abrasions" |
| 2 | negligible | "tiny scratches" |
| 3 | minor | "cuts and bruises" |
| 4 | harmful | "deep cuts" — bleeding starts here |
| 5 | damaging | "deep slashes" |
| 6 | severe | "gaping holes" |
| 7 | devastating | "shattered" |
| 8 | useless | "a stump" |

The four kinds per area: fresh external, external scar, fresh internal, internal scar.

## Where the wordings come from

- `client/client/game/wounds_data.py` is every phrase from Elanthipedia's Damage tables, generated by `tools/wound_tables.py`. Regenerate it; never edit it by hand.
- Where the game's words differ from the wiki's, `CAPTURED` in `wounds.py` holds the game's.
- Anything that matches no wording lands in `Health.unknown`, which `;hunt` echoes.

## Caveats

- Empaths see thirteen levels; HEALTH uses one wording per pair, so the parser collapses to the eight above.
- A wording the wiki lists at two levels is read as the lower one.

## The injuries panel

The game also pushes an injuries panel on every change; the parser keeps it as `injuries` and the Injuries dock draws it. It costs no command but is coarse — a level per part, no kind, no bleeding.

`;hunt` reads the panel first: a clean panel means no HEALTH; a lit part means HEALTH decides how bad. `PANEL_AREAS` maps each panel part to its HEALTH area.

## Bleeding

The Bleeding table becomes a rate per area, with tended and clotted rows at severity 0. An internal bleeder is a separate field (`inside_bleeding`). `;tend` bandages the tendable ones; internal bleeders are left for magic.
