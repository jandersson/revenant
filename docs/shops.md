# Trader shops

Traders rent shops in the Crossing's Market Plaza and sell to anyone there, each shop open on its owner's hours. DRService's [Plaza Shop Browser](https://drservice.info/Plaza/) lists what every shop holds and charges, so an item can be found and priced before walking anywhere.

## The Market Plaza

- 32 rooms off the Foyer (map room 7906, `;go2 7906`), each holding a few shops behind an arch, a door or an entrance.
- The Foyer has a listings board.

## The Plaza Shop Browser

- **Current Items:** every item's tap, shop, city, price in coin and platinum, value and when it was last scanned. An item expands to its read and look text and an Elanthipedia link.
- **Shop List:** each shop's room, portal, owner, open and close hours, and status.
- **Item History** and **Scrolls** have tabs of their own.
- [Export.asmx/Latest](https://drservice.info/Plaza/Export.asmx/Latest) downloads the current items as an Excel file; the page itself loads its table after the fact, so the export is the one to read.
